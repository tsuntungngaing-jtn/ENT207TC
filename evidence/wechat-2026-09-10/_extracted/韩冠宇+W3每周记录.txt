2. Challenges Encountered
Defining a feasible scope. My earlier mobile design covered several daily operational tasks, while dynamic pricing became more central after the industry discussion. I therefore needed to reconsider what could be built and meaningfully examined during the remaining course period. The requirements manual describes continuous monitoring and automatic price changes, but the meeting minutes recommend a smaller demonstration based on data input, analysis, an explained recommendation and human confirmation. I am still working on the exact MVP boundary, including what data will be available and what the demonstration should show.
Reconsidering the interface architecture. A mobile interface was a reasonable starting point for checking daily tasks. Pricing may involve comparing several dates, room types, competitor prices and occupancy figures together. I now think a PC or desktop-oriented dashboard could provide more space for this work. This is my design judgement, not a finding from user testing. The challenge is to connect the business task to the product scope, device and information architecture, rather than simply enlarge the mobile screens.
Taking on coordination responsibilities. As team leader, I need to look beyond my own UX work and understand semester objectives, milestones, individual responsibilities and evidence collection. I have introduced a basic weekly documentation process, but I have not completed the wider task division. Coordinating seven members requires clear ownership without creating unnecessary reporting work. I am currently working out how to make the next tasks and their expected outputs clearer.
