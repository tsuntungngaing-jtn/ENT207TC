Proposal 修改指导和建议
现在的信息源：1. Uno项目介绍。2. EEH—三丁民宿。
我的建议是：多方信息收集并完成整合，团队自我分析后判断方向目标，才能将问题的Scope简化缩小
The Problem
总问题
民宿运营成本高、效率低、规模化能力弱。
根本原因是大量日常运营仍依赖人工重复执行，关键决策又高度依赖个人经验。
具体问题
重复运营工作占用大量人力。
订单确认、房态更新、客户通知等高频任务仍需要人工反复处理。
客服沟通效率低。
客户咨询、入住指引和常见问题需要运营人员持续人工回复。
点评管理耗时且不稳定。
运营人员需要逐条阅读并回复点评，耗费时间，且不同人员的回复质量和风格难以统一。
动态定价高度依赖个人经验。
运营人员难以及时综合入住率、市场需求、节假日和竞争价格进行持续调价。
市场信息分散，决策效率低。
旅游趋势、订单数据、市场需求和竞争信息分布在不同来源，运营人员难以快速整合并形成判断。
运营经验难以沉淀和复制。
关键经营知识主要掌握在有经验的员工手中，难以转化为统一、标准化的运营规则。
人工模式限制规模化扩张。
随着管理民宿数量增加，公司需要同步增加运营人员，导致管理成本和复杂度持续上升。
Overall Problem
Homestay operations remain costly, inefficient, and difficult to scale.
The main reason is that many daily operational tasks still rely heavily on repetitive manual work, while key decisions depend strongly on individual experience.
Specific Problems
Repetitive operational tasks consume significant human resources.
	High-frequency tasks such as order confirmation, room-status updates, and guest notifications still require repeated manual handling.
Guest communication is inefficient.
	Operational staff must repeatedly respond to guest inquiries, provide check-in instructions, and answer common questions.
Review management is time-consuming and inconsistent.
	Staff need to read and respond to guest reviews individually, which takes considerable time and can lead to inconsistent response 	quality and tone.
Dynamic pricing relies heavily on individual experience.
	Operators find it difficult to continuously adjust prices based on occupancy rates, market demand, holidays, and competitor pricing in a 	timely manner.
Market information is fragmented, reducing decision-making efficiency.
	Tourism trends, booking data, market demand, and competitor information are scattered across different sources, making it difficult for 	operators to quickly integrate information and make informed decisions.
Operational knowledge is difficult to retain and replicate.
	Key know-how is often held by experienced staff and is difficult to convert into standardized and reusable operational rules.
The current labor-intensive model limits scalability.
	As the number of managed homestays increases, the company must also increase its operational staff, leading to higher management 	costs and greater operational complexity.

但是我们是在为我们为民宿运营管理公司中的一线运营人员解决高频、重复、信息密集型运营工作的效率问题。
