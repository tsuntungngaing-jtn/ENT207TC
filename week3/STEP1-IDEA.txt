ENT207TC Week 3 — Step 1 · Finalized idea (FuxiPulse)
=====================================================

1) The problem
   Homestay owners in tourist destinations (e.g. Tengchong) face sharp
   occupancy and revenue drops in the weak / shoulder season, but lack a
   clear, timely way to see risk, diagnose causes, and act on OTA pricing
   and promotions before losses compound.

2) Who has the problem
   Independent / small-chain homestay operators and property managers who
   rely on OTAs, have limited revenue-ops staff, and decide under time
   pressure during weak season.

3) The app, and how it solves the problem
   FuxiPulse — an AI operations decision-support dashboard.
   It monitors property pulse + risk, forecasts weak-season demand,
   runs a research agent for root causes, proposes strategies with
   human approval, supports OTA actions, and closes the loop with
   performance feedback / learning.

4) Features (Checkpoint / Draft 1 list)
   - Operations Dashboard — property pulse, risk score, agent pipeline
   - Weak Season Analysis — 14-day forecast + root-cause diagnosis
   - Research Agent — sources · reasoning · findings
   - Strategy Center — recommendations + human approve / reject
   - OTA Operations — channel / listing actions
   - Knowledge Base — playbooks and context
   - Performance Feedback — outcomes and learning loop

Demo property context: Cloud Mist Homestay · Tengchong, Yunnan
