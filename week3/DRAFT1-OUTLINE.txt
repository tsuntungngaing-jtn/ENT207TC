ENT207TC · Draft 1 outline (keep short)
=======================================

01 · The problem
----------------
What: Homestay occupancy and revenue collapse in weak / shoulder season.
Who: Independent and small-chain operators (demo: Cloud Mist Homestay, Tengchong).
Why it matters: Booking pace falls early; without timely diagnosis and OTA action,
losses compound across Ctrip / Meituan / Fliggy. Limited ops staff cannot monitor
all signals manually.

Research note (add 1–2 sources before submit):
- Yunnan / Tengchong tourism seasonality or OTA booking-pace pressure
- Small lodging revenue-management pain points in shoulder season

02 · The solution
-----------------
App: FuxiPulse — AI decision-support for weak-season operations.
How it solves it: Detect risk → forecast & diagnose → research root causes →
propose strategies with human approval → push OTA actions → learn from outcomes.

03 · The features
-----------------
1. Operations Dashboard — property pulse, risk score, agent pipeline status
2. Weak Season Analysis — 14-day demand forecast + root-cause diagnosis
3. Research Agent — sources, reasoning chain, findings
4. Strategy Center — ranked recommendations; approve / reject (human-in-the-loop)
5. OTA Operations — channel share, booking pace, listing rank, monitor actions
6. Knowledge Base — pricing / product / distribution / demand playbooks
7. Performance Feedback — results loop so agents improve next cycle
